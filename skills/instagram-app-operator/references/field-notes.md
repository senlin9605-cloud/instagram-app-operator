# Reusable field notes

## Observed 2026-10-22

- A device preference changed while an open mirror kept showing the original phone. Quitting through the actual menu, selecting the device and relaunching produced a verified switch. Record preference and live connection separately.
- Immediate post-navigation screenshots sometimes retained an old scroll position or captured animation. A second observation exposed the stable layout and prevented targeting a recommendation where a post had appeared.
- An unchanged outer AX tree did not imply an unchanged phone UI; iOS content required visual observation.
- A creator liked and replied to a comment about a specific design detail. This demonstrated a conversation opportunity, not downloads or conversion.
- A newer Reel had more early plays but lower average watch, with no sends, saves or follows. Views alone did not demonstrate better creative. Compare similar ages and retain uncertainty for small samples.
- Distinguish a separately generated concept from an output verified in the advertised App. A popular trend does not establish product fit.
- A user-supplied Chrome screenshot can represent a different signed-in GitHub account from the in-app browser. Inspect the requested browser's actual profile link, not a translated display name or ambient login page, and match the configured repository destination to the verified owner. Git push permission alone does not establish that the destination is the user's intended account.
- A live mirror verified on the intended phone later showed another phone's Home screen, and the app's Settings confirmed a different selected device without a switch from this task. Recheck ownership and pause public actions when this happens; ordinary reconnect authorization does not make repeated device switching safe under conflicting control.
